The workshop is a **half-day event** on **August 17th**, running in two 90-minute blocks
(**14:00 – 15:30** and **16:00 – 17:30**) separated by the conference coffee break, and combining an invited
keynote, contributed paper presentations, and an interactive discussion round.
The workshop will take place in **Room SFG-2030**.
